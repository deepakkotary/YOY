You Only You

Intro : Here we will discuss about absolute world and relative world.
In relative world Everything you see is made for you and by you. If you wake up in a morning with complete  memory 
loss, then you will be supposed to born into a  new world. This means everything was made by you, when 
you will die everything will be gone with you, nothing will be left here.

And at the other side in Absolute world, nothing change with death of you.
